Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Weblance Studios ("Weblance", "we", or "I") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website or contacting us, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out our contact form, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, email address, company or brand name, and details about your project. We also collect basic, non-identifying analytics about how visitors use our site (see "Analytics" below).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, deliver and manage your project, communicate with you, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Analytics

We use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors interact with our site — such as pages viewed, general location, and device type. This information is aggregated and helps us improve the website.

4. Payments

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We do not collect or store your card details — Stripe handles all payment information directly under its own privacy and security policies.

5. Third-Party Services

We rely on a few trusted providers to operate: Google (analytics), Stripe (payments), and Vercel (website hosting). These providers process limited data on our behalf under their own privacy terms.

6. Cookies

Our analytics tools may use cookies to recognize returning visitors and measure site usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time, though some features may not work as intended.

7. Your Privacy Rights (California / CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to request its deletion, and to opt out of the sale of personal information — which we do not do. To make a request, email us using the address below.

8. Data Retention

We keep inquiry and project information only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal or accounting obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

Questions or privacy requests? Email ivan@weblanceagency.com.
