Pricing & Payment

The Starter ($499) is a clean, fast single-page site — perfect if you just need something professional online. The Growth ($1,000) adds more pages, animations, and SEO. The Max ($1,999) is the full package — up to 10 pages, premium effects, and full post-launch support. Check out the pricing page for the full breakdown.

You don't pay until you're happy with the result. Once you approve the final design and it's ready to go live, that's when payment is made. No upfront deposits.
